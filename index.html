Artist Statement

"The Nile runs past
my window."

I grew up in a village in Minya, Upper Egypt — where the river is not a metaphor, it is a neighbor. Where ancient walls speak in three languages at once: Pharaonic, Islamic, Coptic.

I studied at the Faculty of Specific Education, Fine Arts Department, Assiut University, but I have always painted from instinct before theory. I am drawn to heritage — not as nostalgia, but as a living thing.

"النيل يجري أمام نافذتي. نشأت في قرية في المنيا، صعيد مصر — حيث النهر ليس استعارة، بل جار. أسلوبي لم يكتمل بعد — وهذا بالضبط ما يجعل كل لوحة مختلفة عن سابقتها. كل ما أرسمه يبدأ من هنا."
