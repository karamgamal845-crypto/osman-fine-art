Another World

Acrylic on linen fabric · 2026

Artist Statement

The Nile runs past my window.

I grew up in a village in Minya, Upper Egypt — where the river is not a metaphor, it is a neighbor. Where ancient walls speak in three languages at once: Pharaonic, Islamic, Coptic.

I studied at the Faculty of Specific Education, Fine Arts Department, Assiut University, but I have always painted from instinct before theory. I am drawn to heritage — not as nostalgia, but as a living thing. Something still breathing in the mud-brick walls, in the geometry of a minaret, in the face of a farmer at dusk.

My style is not yet complete — and that is precisely what makes every painting different from the one before it.

Everything I paint begins here.
